      About

      Qasryna is a multidisciplinary designer looking to transform ideas into memorable visual experiences. With over 7 years of experience, she has collaborated with clients across a range of industries, from healthcare to tech startups, bringing together graphic design, motion design, and illustration to help brands build cohesive visual identities.

      She holds a BA in Graphic Design with a minor in Animation from Columbia College Chicago and is currently based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

      Always drawing inspiration from the world around her, she strives to create thoughtful designs that balance beauty with purpose.🌏
